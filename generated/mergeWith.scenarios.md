## Timing and scope

Frame 0 is result subscription. Hot input times are relative to that connection; negative times are missed pre-subscription events. Cold times are relative to each input subscription. Even cold frame-0 events here are scheduled after subscription registration; these fixtures do not model synchronous emission inside subscribe. Equal-time delivery follows the fixture scheduler insertion order, not an operator-defined priority. Cancellation fixtures avoid ties. Silent scenarios assert only their finite observation window.
