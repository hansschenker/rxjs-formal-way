## Limits

Finite characterization and bounded comparison are not proofs for all reducers, parameters, payloads or schedules. No arbitrary reentry, cancellation during callbacks, immutable deep snapshots, mutation-event semantics, production interpreter or lossless JSON loader is provided. Mutable-seed aliasing has a separate native characterization test and is outside the immutable differential domain.
